# Disproof of Conjecture 00000000565

The standard coefficient-free A2 cluster exchange graph is exactly a pentagon, so its automorphism group has order 10. Its Weyl group, generated by the two simple reflections, has order 6. Lagrange prevents an injective copy of this Weyl group in the exchange-graph automorphism group; therefore no semidirect product W(A2) ⋊ H can describe it, for any group H or action.

## Scope

Uses the customary unlabelled cluster exchange graph of finite type, with vertices clusters and edges mutations. The source supplies no alternative formal definition. The full symbolic A2 mutation component is reconstructed, not assumed. This disproves the finite-type assertion and therefore the statement as written; it makes no claim about a separately corrected affine formulation. The A2 pentagon is classical and is explicitly attributed.

## Formalization

Actual rational functions live in FractionRing (MvPolynomial (Fin 2) ℚ). Lean proves distinctness, all exchange identities, an exact two-successor mutation classification, and completeness for arbitrary finite mutation sequences. It identifies the actual reachable-state automorphism group with the finite permutation subgroup preserving the cycle and computes order 10 in the kernel. The simple-root reflection formula is checked, the generated Weyl subgroup has order 6, and the final theorem excludes a semidirect-product isomorphism for every group H and action. The specialization of general seed mutation to the coefficient-free A2 rule is explained explicitly in the paper.
